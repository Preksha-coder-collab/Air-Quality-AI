About Our Project

AI Powered Air Quality Prediction System using Machine Learning and Environmental Data

Problem Statement

Air pollution is one of the most serious environmental challenges affecting public health, ecosystems and quality of life.

Many people are unaware of harmful air conditions until they begin experiencing health issues. Traditional monitoring systems provide limited forecasting and delayed information.

Our Solution

Machine Learning

Random Forest algorithm predicts AQI using environmental parameters.

AQI Forecasting

Predict future AQI levels and identify pollution risks.

Health Awareness

Provide actionable insights to reduce health impacts.
